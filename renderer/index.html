Connect to Gemini

  1. Paste your Gemini API key below.
  2. Choose Check connection to verify the key and model.
  3. Choose Save settings, then start either translation direction.

Your spoken language is detected automatically. Each direction has its own target language on the main screen.

Gemini connection

Checks the entered key, model and endpoint without saving them or sending audio.

Gemini receives audio when you start translation or a sample test. Normal audio stays on your computer.

Latency & session tuning

0 seconds disables the silence timeout. Each direction stops independently and returns to normal audio.

The queue limit drops older translated audio when delayed. Network and model latency vary.

Audio system & virtual devices

Dependency checks leave current audio running. Choose a backend before joining a call: applying one recreates virtual devices and restarts both routes in normal mode. Reselect GeminiMic and GeminiSpeaker in your meeting afterward. Checks stay local.

Meet uses GeminiMic as its microphone and GeminiSpeaker as its speaker. Check that both virtual cables and your physical audio devices are ready before joining a call.

Two independent virtual cables are required. Rename the meeting-facing input to GeminiMic and the meeting-facing output to GeminiSpeaker where the OS/driver permits. Device names shown in Meet are controlled by the driver.

This mode uses installed drivers. Their devices remain installed when the app closes. On Windows, use two VB-CABLE endpoints (for example Cable A and B). On macOS, use two separately named BlackHole devices. One shared cable cannot isolate both directions. Third-party drivers are not bundled.

Saving connection or tuning changes stops translation and keeps normal audio flowing. Press Start again to translate.
